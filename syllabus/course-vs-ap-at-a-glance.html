All of it on a real, local machine.

Most CS courses run in a browser sandbox. Here, students install Python, manage real files, use the real terminal, capture real network traffic, and version their own code with Git, on the same laptop all year. The computer itself is part of the curriculum.

About the AP track: the AP layer is opt-in per student and exam-free by default. Students who opt in prepare through a free, College Board-endorsed online curriculum alongside class and may sit the AP CSP exam in May; everyone else takes the same course and earns a full Computer Science I credit with no exam. AP topic coverage is documented topic by topic in the course's coverage map.

Details of outside services, AP requirements, fees, and dates change; verify current specifics with the instructor.
