Students work on a real local computer, not a browser sandbox: they install Python, manage files, use the terminal, capture network traffic, and version their code with Git on the same laptop all year. The computer itself is part of the curriculum.

About the AP track: the AP layer is opt-in per student and exam-free by default. Students who opt in prepare through a free, College Board-endorsed online curriculum alongside class and may sit the AP CSP exam in May; everyone else takes the same course and earns a full Computer Science I credit with no exam. The base course touches every AP topic at a high level; touching a topic is not the same as teaching it to exam depth, and exam depth is what the AP track's self-paced curriculum provides. Coverage is documented topic by topic in the course's coverage map.

Details of outside services, AP requirements, fees, and dates change; verify current specifics with the instructor.
